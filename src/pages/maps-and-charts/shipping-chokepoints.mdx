After the Iran war, the Strait of Hormuz became a major chokepoint for shipping of brent crude out of the Persian Gulf.
<br/>
The worldwide sea is dotted with similar chokepoints. These are narrow passages or capes where shipping traffic is forced through in order to avoid long rerouting.
<br/>
This map uses data from the Humanitarian Data Exchange, an UN initiative, to show where these crucial chokepoints are. Circles are sized based on monthly average capacity through these straits.
