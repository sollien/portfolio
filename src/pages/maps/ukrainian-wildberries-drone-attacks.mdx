In July and August 2026, Ukraine launched a series of drone attacks targeting Wildberries warehouses in Russia. Wildberries are in many ways the Russian equivalent of Amazon—a large e-commerce company that ships consumer goods.
<br/>
Ukraine has claimed that the warehouses were being used to ship drone components, navigational systems and other military equipment. At least 19 warehouses have been struck.
<br/>
Drones were presumably launched from different parts of Ukrainian territory. Each attack was carried out with a flurry of drones, sometimes involving hundreds of drones.
<br/>
The map shows the locations of the warehouses, symbolized by squares color graded by date of attack. Arrows were added to signify the place of origin for the attacks. Attacks were on-going at the time of creation, and thus the temporality of the map is restricted to that timespan. More warehouses may have been struck since the map was published.
